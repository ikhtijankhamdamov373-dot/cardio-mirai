Prototype Scope

Cardio MIRAI combines structured clinical screening with an ECG intake workflow. Production ECG interpretation would require validated signal processing, audited models, clinical governance, and secure storage.

Clinical variables

Age, sex, BMI, waist, blood pressure, smoking, diabetes, family history, symptoms, ECG flags, LVEF, LA size, and E/e'.

ECG formats

Designed for scanned ECGs and research records inspired by PTB-XL workflows: waveform data, header files, and metadata tables.

Official web identity

The page is branded for cardiomirai.uz and can be hosted as a static website because it is pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Medical disclaimer: Cardio MIRAI is a research and demonstration prototype. It is not a medical device and must not be used as a substitute for physician assessment, diagnosis, emergency care, or treatment decisions.
